OpenHop companion channels

WXDispatch creates hash channels only. Names must begin with #, and MeshCore deterministically derives the channel key from the name. Anyone who knows the exact channel name can derive its key. Operations accepted by the MeshCore companion are read back for metadata verification. Companion acceptance does not confirm over-air delivery.
